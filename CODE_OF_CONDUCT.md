# Code of Conduct

moodle-cli follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). This page summarises how it applies here; where the two differ, the Covenant wins.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and assume good faith. Most contributors are students using the tool for their own studies.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and say so when you got something wrong.
- Respect privacy. Do not post another person's Moodle data, grades, messages or identity, and do not ask others to share theirs.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or sexualised language and imagery.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, and deliberately derogatory comments.
- Publishing someone else's private information, such as an email address, student id or Moodle content, without their explicit permission.
- Using this project or its issue tracker to help anyone breach academic integrity rules or access accounts that are not theirs.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every project space, including issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at **bunizaoccc@gmail.com**. Reports are handled privately, and the reporter's identity is kept confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainer will review every report and respond in a way they judge fair, following the Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines). Depending on how serious and how repeated the behaviour is, that ranges from a private warning to a temporary or permanent ban from the project. Comments, commits, code and issues that break this code may be edited or removed.
